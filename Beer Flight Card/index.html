THE TAPROOM

A 52-Card Beer Flight Game

Taproom House Rules

OBJECTIVE

Win 3 Shifts (rounds). To win a Shift, build the highest-scoring Beer Flight before Last Call finishes.

THE TURN

  1. The Serve: Start with 4 cards. Choose ONE action:
    • Pour a Brew (2–10): Place onto your Paddle. (Top-Off: If you have 4 Brews, play a higher Brew to replace your lowest one).
    • Call a Patron (J, Q, K, A): Discard for its effect.
    • Pour Out (Waste): Discard 1 unwanted card.
  2. The Refill: Draw from the Keg until you hold 4 cards again.

LAST CALL & THE KEG

  • Last Call: You must have exactly 4 Brews in your flight to ring Last Call. Once rung, the shift ends and the highest score wins.
  • The Keg Kicks: If the 52-card deck runs out before anyone rings Last Call, the shift ends immediately and all Flight Bonuses are voided.
