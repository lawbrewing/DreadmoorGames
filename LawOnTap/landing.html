🍻 The Perfect Pour

Tap and hold the metal handles at the top of the screen to pour Marshmallow Stout, nIPLy, or LFO. Release in the target zone for a Perfect Pour and a combo multiplier.

ONE SHOT ONLY: If you let go of the tap too early, the drink is instantly trashed. No topping off!

🤬 The Karen Gamble

Karen never knows what she wants. When she steps to the bar, just pour *something*. You have a 33% chance to guess right and earn a massive +200 Tip. Guess wrong, and suffer Karen Rage (lose 100 tips).

📋 House Recipe Cheat Sheet

Starting on Shift 3, patrons will order layered drinks. Keep an eye on the order board!

Black & Tan: LFO (50%) + Marshmallow Stout (50%)
Black & Bitter: nIPLy (50%) + Marshmallow Stout (50%)
Lawnmower Hop: LFO (50%) + nIPLy (50%)

⚖️ The Judge

At the end of every shift, The Judge demands a massive flight of beers on a wooden paddle. Nail every single glass to level up.

WARNING: Trash his flight, and you suffer a brutal 1,500 tip penalty and must repeat the shift!
