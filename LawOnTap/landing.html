LAW ON TAP

Survive the Shift. Master the Pour.

Tap to Start

🍻 The Perfect Pour

Tap and hold the metal handles at the top of the screen to pour Stout, IPA, or Lager. Release in the target zone for a Perfect Pour and a combo multiplier.

ONE SHOT ONLY: If you let go of the tap too early, the drink is instantly trashed. No topping off!

🧪 Layered Drinks

VIPs and Bosses might order mixed drinks like a Black & Tan or a Lawnmower Hop. Watch the order card carefully and switch to the second tap when the bottom half is full to layer it perfectly.

🤬 The Karen Gamble

Karen never knows what she wants. When she steps to the bar, just pour *something*. You have a 33% chance to guess right and earn a massive +200 Tip. Guess wrong, and suffer Karen Rage (lose 100 tips).

⚖️ The Judge

At the end of every shift, The Judge arrives. He demands a massive flight of beers on a wooden paddle. Nail every single glass in his flight to level up and earn a life back.

WARNING: Trash his flight, and you suffer a brutal 1,500 tip penalty and must repeat the shift!
